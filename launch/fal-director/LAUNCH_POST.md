## Follow-up with detail and credit request

Why Director? Learner choices can become new directions while the same video session continues. In this take, Aoi notices the date and puts away a clue. The recorder sent two directions; the second was accepted, but its scene fell beyond the captured playback.

Japanese speech and captions are added by the app for readable, controlled learning content. The next step is richer branching dialogue and better synchronization between choices, captions, and incoming scenes.

@fal — I'd love to be considered for the H3 Max Director builder credits to develop this further. The working local capture code, prompts, assets, and run report are public in the repo.
